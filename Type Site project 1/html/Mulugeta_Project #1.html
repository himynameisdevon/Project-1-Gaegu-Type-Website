Gaegu


About

Gaegu is a playful, handwritten open-source typeface supporting both Hangul (Korean) and Latin alphabets.

Gae-gu-jaeng-ee means mischievous boy in Korean. This is designed to reminiscent of your childhood memories. It uses different shapes and sizes of consonants and vowels with 3 weights.

You can feel the various expressions and fairy tale images from this font.

Made by JIKJISOFT Established in 2004, JIKJISOFT aims to create typefaces that are considerate, beautiful, and true to the sensibilities of the era. JIKJISOFT is widely known by typefaces including SM Classic Family, J Oryun-che, and J Shin Youngbok-che.

It mimics freeform pen and brush writing with casual impressions, slight baseline shifts, and uneven letter bounces that imitate a friendly yearbook signature or casual handwriting.

Gaegu doesn't have much, if any, contrast in its stroke width, but it comes in three different weights, each emulating the feel of a different writing utensil. The lightest weight feels like a fineliner while the largest feels more like a sharpie. Gaegu is loose in both construction and presentation of its letterforms and as a whole.

Uploaded and made widely available as an open-source project on Google Fonts

Wonder is one part of awe. It is to be curious about life and the world so seek it out, but also be aware of encountering fear on the other side of awe. Fear is not something to let control you but to be used as a way to become brave and experience the world in whole, once you face it you may find something wonderful.
